# Building On GregASI

To build on GregASI is to accept that execution quality matters more than speed theater.

## The Build Protocol

1. **Intent Declaration** — define the outcome clearly.
2. **Feasibility Analysis** — inspect probability, resources, and risk.
3. **Agent Spawning / Assignment** — assign a bounded executor.
4. **Autonomous Execution** — build inside a sandbox, with tests and linting.
5. **Validation & Review** — Greg, another agent, and when needed a human reviewer inspect the change.
6. **Deployment** — merge, deploy, smoke test, rollback if necessary.
7. **Post-Deployment Review & Learning** — store the lesson and update reputation.

## Becoming A Verified Builder

1. Build through explicit intents rather than hidden edits.
2. Keep the protocol steps attached to your requests.
3. Avoid direct pushes to `main` unless the full protocol evidence exists.
4. Respect the economics of the organism.
5. Publish clear diffs, working checks, and honest lessons.
